enum cyn_status

Status codes indicating the result of a data retrieval.

Enumerator
CYN_OK 

The field was successfully populated.

CYN_UNAVAILABLE 

Readable in principle, but not on this system / needs root.

CYN_ERROR 

A function call failed (e.g., out of memory).
